Rune's Journal Cipher

NOTE: at time of initial release, all text will be converted to lower case. It just makes life a helluvalot easier. So you'll need to manually add your capital letters back in once the ciphertext has been spat out. (That happens on the next page, and the button to navigate to it will be visible once you've generated a cipher table.)

Also, J's get turned into I's at every turn, so a J in the keyword will become an I for the purposes of generating the cipher.
